Project 01 · TSYP 13 · 2025

去中心化多机器人配送车队

AntRoute

Decentralized multi-agent fleet coordination with bio-inspired pheromone mapping and fairness-aware auctions.

  • 1st Place · IEEE RAS & VTS SmartFleet Challenge
  • Team of 2
  • Simulation + hardware prototype

问题The problem

Last-mile delivery accounts for more than half of total shipping cost.

Autonomous fleets can help, but most fleet systems use one central controller. If that controller fails, the whole fleet stops, and it becomes a communication bottleneck as the fleet grows. Many decentralized methods only optimize speed: some vehicles get overloaded while others sit idle, and outer neighborhoods get worse service.

方案What we built

AntRoute is a decentralized coordination system for mixed fleets of trucks, ground robots and drones. Vehicles communicate only with nearby vehicles and decide among themselves who handles each delivery.

  • 500 mneighbour radio range
  • 2 smessage interval
  • ~6 sto detect a failed vehicle
  • 30 sroute replanning cycle

核心思路Three ideas

01 · 数字信息素

Digital pheromone mapping

Each vehicle keeps a local map of delivery demand, congestion and priority, updated through decay, diffusion and exchanges with neighbours. Unlike classic Ant Colony Optimization, pheromone builds up where new requests are likely to appear, so the fleet positions itself before demand arrives.

02 · 公平性拍卖

Fairness-aware auctions

Vehicles bid on each task with a score weighing travel time, battery cost and fairness: how much work the vehicle has already done and how well the destination area has been served. Emergency medical deliveries skip the fairness rules and are assigned immediately.

03 · 协同故障恢复

Collaborative failure recovery

Neighbours detect a failed vehicle within about 6 seconds when its status messages stop, then run an emergency auction for its tasks. When 3 or more tasks are left without a vehicle, the fleet sets up a virtual swap station at a meeting point to hand packages over.

系统架构Each vehicle runs four layers at the same time

  1. L1Pheromone mappingdecay · diffusion · neighbour exchange
  2. L2Auction-based task allocationtime · battery · fairness score
  3. L3Adaptive routingmodified A* weighted by pheromone & congestion, replanned every 30 s
  4. L4Resilience monitoringheartbeat loss → emergency auction

硬件原型Hardware prototype

To test the idea beyond simulation we built two small 3D-printed robots on wheels:

  • Each robot: an ESP32, two DC motors and a motor driver, running C++ firmware written with Arduino.
  • Robots connect over WiFi to a Node.js WebSocket server that starts each auction and relays messages between robots.
  • Each robot calculates its own bid from its distance and energy level, drives to pickup and drop-off, estimates its position from wheel speeds and streams position, energy and pheromone data in real time.
  • A React + HTML5 Canvas web simulator shows the fleet live, including auctions, pheromone trails, A* paths, failure injection and live metrics.

Project 02 · IoT · Health · 2026

阿尔茨海默症患者智能辅助系统

Smart Assistance System for Alzheimer’s Patients

AR smart glasses, the MEMO-BAND health bracelet, a backend server and a caregiver mobile app. Hardware, firmware, backend and app, built end to end.

  • Working prototypes
  • Academic prototype → startup product in development

问题The problem

Help people with Alzheimer’s live more safely and independently, while family follows their condition in real time.

The patient needs help remembering who they are talking to and where they are, and someone needs to know immediately when they fall, their vital signs drift or they leave a safe area.

方案What I built

An IoT system with four parts that talk over Wi-Fi. The glasses tell the patient who is in front of them; the bracelet watches vital signs and falls on-device; the backend does the recognition; the app puts it all in a caregiver’s pocket.

How the AR display works. The mirror reflects the text from the OLED onto the transparent glass, so the patient sees the information on top of the real world, like a simple head-up display.

Face recognition. The camera sends images over Wi-Fi to the backend, which recognizes family members and friends. The glasses then show the person’s name and relation, so the patient remembers who they are talking to.

Room recognition. The backend also recognizes which room the patient is in, for example the kitchen: visual indoor localization, called vSLAM in the project.

Project 03 · Sagemcom internship · Summer 2026

工业视觉检测与监控系统

AXIS

Real-time supervision of a PCB inspection cell, and a deep-learning inspector that sees a defect the line’s industrial sensor could not.

  • Sole developer
  • 2 months
  • 17-page technical report to the Test Development department

问题The problem

A metal shield on the board must be pressed flat, not just present. An edge lifted 1–3 mm leaks radiation and makes the board non-conforming.

The line’s Cognex Checker 4G7 gave its verdict only as hard-wired on/off outputs: no history, no timestamps, no stored image, no traceability and no cause, just “good / not good”.

三种状态Three states to separate

StateLooks likeDecision
OKshield placed and pressed flataccept
Looseright place, one edge lifted 1–3 mmreject
Missingnothing on the footprintreject

第一部分 · 实时监控Part 1 · Supervision, validated on real hardware

  1. Cognex Checker 4G72 inspection sensors + ladder logic → outputs D0 / D1
  2. Siemens S7-1200wired to I1.0 / I1.2, copied into DB1 (TIA Portal V17)
  3. Snap7 · 500 msPython reads one status byte over S7comm
  4. FastAPIone row per board in SQLite, FTP server receives the images
  5. WebSocketpushes every state change to the browser
  6. Dashboardoperator / admin roles, bcrypt + signed session cookie

The data source sits behind one interface switched by a single setting (mock · real · vision). When inspection later moved from the Cognex sensor to a webcam, no line of the dashboard, storage or real-time transport had to be rewritten.

Live console · real Cognex image via FTP

关键发现The discovery · a defect invisible from above

Running it on real boards exposed the real problem: the Cognex sensor only caught a missing shield. A shield placed but not pressed flat passed as good.

Top-down image
bright, specular shield
Cognex Checker
pass
AXIS model
OK

The reference case: shield placed and pressed flat.

Proved by measurement, not tuning: a 1.5 mm lift at 150 mm working distance is about a 1 % scale change, under 2 pixels on a 376-pixel-wide image. A tilted edge, though, deflects the specular reflection away from the lens and the region goes dark. That signal exists in the pixels, but the Checker only offers zone thresholds and no pixel access. The fix was a different camera, not a better setting.

第二部分 · 视觉重建Part 2 · Rebuilding the inspection in Python

  1. USB webcamMicrosoft LifeCam HD-3000, 720p: full pixel access
  2. Board normalisationgreen-PCB mask → morphology → min-area rectangle → homography to a 1100 × 592 canonical view, with guards against stray green blobs
  3. ResNet-18pre-trained, only the last block and output layer fine-tuned on 300 real images (100 per class, 4 sessions)
  4. Verdict with a causeOK / loose / missing goes into the database, WebSocket and display, not just a boolean
  5. Right to abstainconfidence < 0.55 or brightness outside the trained 72–99 range → INDETERMINATE, nothing written
Left: board found in frame · Right: rectified view, ROI, class probabilities

实验结果Result · one whole capture session held out for testing

  • 93%overall accuracy on the held-out session
  • 100%on missing shield
  • 82%on loose shield, the defect the Cognex missed
  • 7 msper image

已知局限Known limits, measured

The main weakness is absolute lighting: drift between sessions (19.2 grey levels) and moving the board in the field (18.5) shift the signal more than the defect itself (15.3). All four sessions come from the same morning, so scores are optimistic. Until diffuse lighting and relative photometric input are added, the lighting guard detects the condition and suspends the verdict.
